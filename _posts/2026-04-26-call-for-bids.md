## Call for Bids: 13th Global Wordnet Conference (January 2027)

The **Global Wordnet Association** welcomes bids from potential organizers of the **13th Global Wordnet Conference**, to be held in **January 2027**.

- **Deadline for proposals:** **15 May 2026** (we apologize for the short notice)  
- **Decision announced:** **22 May 2026**

### Proposal criteria

- **Expected size:** 50–100 people

- **Logistics**
  - Convenient and reasonably priced international flight connections 
  - Inexpensive lodging for participants on small budgets

- **Date**
  - Projected date: January 2027
  - Conference length of 4 days

- **Participants / Content**
  - Efforts to expand participation beyond the current wordnet community are encouraged
  - Co-located tutorials, workshops, panels, hackathons, shared tasks, and other events are welcome

- **Proceedings**
  - Local organizers act as main editors
  - Proceedings must obtain an ISBN and be freely downloadable from the GWA website
  - Proceedings should be registered in the ACL Anthology (the GWA Board will assist)

- **Budget and Fees** *(model – variations may be proposed)*
  - Aim to keep the regular participant fee at **€200 or less** (lower is better)
  - Reduced fee for students and participants from under-funded countries
  - Fees should cover:
    - Proceedings (electronic & ISBN)
    - Coffee breaks
    - Lunches (depending on local costs)
  - Fees do **not necessarily** cover:
    - Accommodation
    - Dinners
    - Local transport to the venue
  - Optional additional fees may cover: banquet, excursion

- **Costs borne by local organizer**
  - Meeting rooms and equipment rental
  - Local support staff

- **Sponsorship**
  - Organizers are encouraged to seek sponsors
  - Invited speaker(s) from the international community to be selected in consultation with the GWA Board

- **Board and local organizers**
  - Number of invited speakers depends on available financial support
  - Local organizers may additionally invite and sponsor local speakers in consultation with the GWA Board

- **Financial responsibility**
  - The GWA cannot contribute financially or assume financial responsibility
  - Financial responsibility rests entirely with the local organizer
  - Early registration is encouraged to reduce risk
  - Require at least one early-registered author per accepted paper for inclusion in the proceedings

- **Program Committee**
  - GWA Board members
  - Co-chairs: the GWA Chair and one local candidate proposed by organizers (ratified by the Board)
  - others to be invited by the program chairs.
  - Best paper award planned
  - Program Committee to be formed **before** the Call for Papers
