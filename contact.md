# Contact

We encourage you to share your questions, comments, and ideas with us!
Join the conversation on our discussion forum at
[https://github.com/orgs/globalwordnet/discussions](https://github.com/orgs/globalwordnet/discussions). Your
feedback is invaluable, and we look forward to engaging with you to
foster collaboration and improve our resources.

### Description

wordnet@linguistlist.org is an open, lightly moderated mailing list for anyone who builds, studies, or uses wordnets and related lexical‑semantic resources. It provides a single forum for sharing releases, tools, research questions, and CFPs, as well as discussing best practices in creating, linking, and using wordnets across languages and domains. Posts should stay on topic, be concise and respectful, and link to large files rather than attaching them; all messages are archived publicly for the benefit of the global community.

More information on different wordnet projects and general documentation can also be found online <https://globalwordnet.org/>.  Questions about individual wordnets may be better addressed to the project in question, if you are not sure, feel free to ask here.
